We are super excited to let you know what's coming in 2027. In November of that year, we will have the incredible opportunity to travel together to the Holy Land and beyond, walking the very places where the Bible comes alive. This will be a journey through Israel, Jordan (including Petra), and Greece, visiting the lands where the patriarchs, prophets, Jesus, and the apostles lived, taught, and ministered. It's one thing to read the Scriptures; it's another thing entirely to stand where these events actually took place.

Calvary Chapel Daily Manna and Regeneration Calvary Chapel are partnering together and traveling with expert guides who know both the land and the Word of God well. At each location, we'll take time for sound, expository biblical teaching, helping us see Scripture with fresh eyes and deeper understanding. Your safety, spiritual growth, and overall experience are very important to us. This trip isn't about rushing through sites; it's about slowing down, opening the Bible, and letting the Lord minister to us in a powerful and unforgettable way.

We want to personally invite you to prayerfully consider joining us on what truly will be a once-in-a-lifetime journey, experiencing the history, culture, food, and traditions of these remarkable places through the lens of Scripture and in the fellowship of our church family.

Day 2 · Exploring Athens

  • The Acropolis
  • Mars Hill

Tour of Israel & Jordan · November 2–12, 2027

Day1
Tue · Nov 2 · Depart USA
Travel to Tel Aviv, Israel.
Day2

Day3

Mt. Carmel, Megiddo, the Nazareth Precipice, lunch at the Sea of Galilee (Decks) and the Yardenit Baptism Site. Dinner and overnight in Tiberias.
Day4

A boat ride on the Sea of Galilee, the Boat Museum, Caesarea Philippi, Tel Dan, the Mt. of Beatitudes, Tabgha and Capernaum. Dinner and overnight in Tiberias.

Beit She'an, cross into Jordan at the Sheikh Hussein Bridge, visit Jerash and Mt. Nebo, then an afternoon at leisure at the Dead Sea hotel in Jordan.

The Temple Mount, the Western (Wailing) Wall, the Rabbinical Tunnels, the Pool of Bethesda, the Antonia Fortress, the Via Dolorosa, the Church of the Holy Sepulchre and the Jaffa Gate. Overnight in Jerusalem.
Day10
Thu · Nov 11 · City of David & Bethlehem
The City of David, Hezekiah's Tunnel, the Davidson Center and Bethlehem. Farewell dinner in Jerusalem and overnight.
Day11

Fly back to the USA, or continue on to the Greece extension.

What do you need?

  • A valid passport, current for at least six months after our trip.
    • If you don't hold a United States passport, you may need a visa from the embassies of the nations we visit.
  • Comfortable walking shoes. Plan on walking 7,000–10,000 steps a day.
  • Comfortable, lightweight clothing. The climate will be similar to Southern California.
    • Bring a light jacket or raincoat, and a hat or cap.
  • Ladies: bring a head scarf for visits to Muslim-controlled areas.
    • No bare shoulders
    • No short skirts
  • Men: bring "Columbia-type" pants with zip-off legs (a pair of long pants is good too).
    • At the holy sites we have to cover our legs.
  • When packing, less is more.
  • All medications in a zip-lock bag.
  • An EpiPen, if needed.
